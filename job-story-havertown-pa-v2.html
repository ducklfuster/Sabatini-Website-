The challenge

The homeowners wanted a crew that could handle detailed trim work and interior painting in the same visit, not one or the other. Their hallway had two separate problems stacked on top of each other: outdated popcorn texture on the ceilings, and old wallpaper adhesive on the walls that had never been sealed properly. Before any finish work could start, both surfaces needed real repair — not a quick skim and repaint.

What we did

  • Scraped and skim-coated the ceilings. Popcorn texture came off entirely, replaced with a smooth, modern surface.
  • Skim-coated the stairwell and hallway walls. Eliminated the uneven texture left behind by old wallpaper glue.
  • Installed new crown molding. Added architectural detail that wasn't there before.
  • Painted as one cohesive space. Stairwell, hallway, and living room finished together, not as three separate rooms.

The result

The hallway, stairwell, and living room now share one smooth, cohesive finish, ceilings included — no visible seams from the old glue, no dated texture, trim sharp enough to notice.
