CHALLENGE

The homeowners wanted a crew that could handle detailed trim work and interior painting in the same visit, not one or the other. Their hallway had two separate problems stacked on top of each other: outdated popcorn texture on the ceilings, and old wallpaper adhesive on the walls that had never been sealed properly. Before any finish work could start, both surfaces needed real repair — not a quick skim and repaint.

OUR APPROACH

We scraped the popcorn texture off the ceilings and applied a full skim coat for a smooth, modern surface. The stairwell and hallway walls got the same skim-coat treatment to eliminate the uneven texture left by the old wallpaper glue. New crown molding went in for architectural detail. Then the stairwell, hallway, and living room were painted as one cohesive space, not three separate rooms.

RESULT

The hallway, stairwell, and living room now share one smooth, cohesive finish, ceilings included — no visible seams from the old glue, no dated texture, trim sharp enough to notice. Project details below are exactly what went on the walls, recorded for the homeowners' records.
