Layer 2 · Professional protection

Nothing left to memory or a handshake.

Most problems on a job aren't disasters — they're misunderstandings. So we put the whole thing in writing and in photos, start to finish.

This protects your project
  • A written proposal, and written change orders if anything shifts
  • Clear, daily communication — you always know what's happening and what's next
  • Every day, documented with time-stamped photos and shared with you in a custom end-of-day report
  • A final walkthrough against a written checklist — our crew leader signs off on every item, then we ask you to sign off too, so nothing gets called "done" until you agree it is
  • A written warranty of three to five years, depending on your product selection — if it isn't holding up, we come back and make it right

Before you hire anyone

Five things every homeowner should verify.

Whether you hire us or not, ask everyone who wants to work in your home:

  • 01Do they carry workers' compensation insurance? (Ask for a current certificate.)
  • 02Do they carry general liability insurance — and is it current?
  • 03Do they use subcontractors — and if so, are those subs covered too?
  • 04Is there a written warranty, in writing, before work begins?
  • 05Do they have real references and reviews you can actually check?

Even if you don't hire us, we hope this page helps you ask sharper questions and hire with confidence. That's the whole point.
