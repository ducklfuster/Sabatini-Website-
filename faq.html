How far do you travel for jobs?

Up to 60 minutes from Folsom, PA, with a preferred radius of about 40 minutes. Jobs further out still get quoted — they just go through a slightly more thorough qualification step before we schedule.

Do you offer a warranty?

Yes — every job ends with a written warranty, not just a verbal promise. It's part of the final walkthrough, not an upsell.

What paint brands and sheens do you use?

Mostly Sherwin-Williams and Benjamin Moore, matched to the job — semi-gloss on trim, satin or eggshell on walls, depending on the room and how it's used. You'll get a dated color guide afterward listing exactly what went where.

What's the "Green Sheet"?

Our pre-price intake process — real questions about your routine, parking, furniture, and anything else that affects how the crew works in your space, asked before we ever talk price.

Will you protect my floors and furniture?

Every time, on every job — drop cloths, masking, and daily cleanup, not just on the last day.

How do I get a quote?

Fill out the short form on our Get Quote page — we'll follow up quickly with next steps and a time to walk the property.
